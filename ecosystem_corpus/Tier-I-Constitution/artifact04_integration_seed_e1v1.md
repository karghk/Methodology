PURPOSE

The Integration Seed preserves the minimum complete identity
required to restore the ecosystem.

Its purpose is to allow reconstruction without depending on
the original operational environment.

It preserves:

• ecosystem identity

• artifact orientation

• foundational reference

• structural entry point

============================================================
FOUNDATIONAL POSITION
============================================================

The synchronized restoration model:

Methodological Foundation

↓

Atlas

↓

Integration Seed

↓

Artifact Ecosystem

↓

Operational Environment

The Integration Seed does not contain the Methodological
Foundation.

It preserves the references required to restore awareness of
it.

============================================================
INTEGRATION PRINCIPLE
============================================================

A seed does not contain the entire ecosystem.

A seed contains the conditions necessary for the ecosystem to
regenerate.

Therefore, the Integration Seed preserves:

identity before expansion,

orientation before operation,

structure before activity.
