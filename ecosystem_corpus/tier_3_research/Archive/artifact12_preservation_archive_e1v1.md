PURPOSE

The Preservation Archive maintains the long-term continuity of
the ecosystem.

Its purpose is to preserve:

• recognized structures

• historical transitions

• qualification records

• synchronization states

• recovery information

The Preservation Archive ensures that ecosystem evolution
does not become ecosystem loss.
