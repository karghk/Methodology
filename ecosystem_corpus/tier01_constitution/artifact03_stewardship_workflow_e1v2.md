PURPOSE

The Stewardship Workflow defines the operational sequence
through which stewardship maintains ecosystem integrity.

Its purpose is to transform principles into repeatable action.

It governs:

• review

• examination

• evaluation

• decision

• preservation

The Workflow does not create legitimacy.

It applies legitimacy standards.

============================================================
WORKFLOW PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Stewardship is not control.

Stewardship is responsible continuity.

The workflow exists to answer:

"How do we preserve, examine, and evolve the ecosystem
without distorting its foundations?"

============================================================
OPERATIONAL DRIFT OBSERVATION PROTOCOL
============================================================

The Operational Drift Observation Protocol is recognized as
a distinct operational mechanism operating within the
Stewardship Workflow relationship.

Its purpose is to observe, record, classify, and appropriately
contain material operational deviation without collapsing
observation into interpretation or granting the observation
mechanism independent methodological authority.

The protocol is distinct from the AI Baseline Component.

The AI Baseline Component provides AI-instance operational
orientation.

The Operational Drift Observation Protocol provides an
operational observation mechanism for identifying and preserving
material deviation from the established operational state.

The protocol is also distinct from OSRA.

OSRA provides verified-state re-anchoring.

The Operational Drift Observation Protocol provides observation
of operational deviation.

The protocol may produce or support observations preserved
through the Observation Register, but it does not become the
Observation Register itself.

The protocol does not:

• redefine constitutional truth

• create independent methodological authority

• replace the Stewardship Workflow

• replace OSRA

• become the AI Baseline Component

• establish universal AI behavior

• establish universal protocol effectiveness

• justify an Instantiation Core by itself

• alter the canonical artifact numbering boundary

Observation remains distinct from interpretation, derivation,
hypothesis, assessment, candidate formation, and recognition.

The protocol therefore operates as a subordinate operational
mechanism under the existing Stewardship Domain and preserves
the authority boundaries of the canonical ecosystem.

Relationship:

↓

The protocol's canonical recognition does not make its
observations self-authenticating or self-authorizing.

OPERATIONAL EXECUTION DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Major operational processes shall maintain one Active
Objective.

objective shall reach a recognized completion state or enter a
recognized paused, frozen, or blocked state.

Operational State Snapshots.

============================================================
AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
============================================================

Stewardship Workflow may:

• organize operations

• execute reviews

• initiate qualification

• preserve continuity

• recognize subordinate operational mechanisms when the
  applicable qualification and authority conditions are met

Stewardship Workflow may not:

• redefine constitutional truth

• elevate candidates automatically

• modify history

• replace methodology

The recognition of the Operational Drift Observation Protocol
is therefore an operational-governance recognition and does not
alter the authority hierarchy of the Methodology ecosystem.
