PURPOSE

Stewardship Review evaluates the condition, integrity, and
alignment of the ecosystem.

It determines whether the ecosystem remains:

• coherent

• legitimate

• preserved

• aligned with its constitutional boundaries

Stewardship Review does not create authority.

It assesses the condition of existing authority and structure.

============================================================
FOUNDATIONAL POSITION
============================================================

The ecosystem recognizes:

Methodological Foundation

↓

↓

The Methodological Foundation provides the examination
principles.

Stewardship Review performs the evaluation.

============================================================
ROLE OF STEWARDSHIP REVIEW
============================================================

Stewardship Review exists to answer:

"Is the ecosystem still operating according to its
recognized principles?"

It evaluates:

1.

Structural integrity.

2.

Constitutional alignment.

3.

Methodological alignment.

4.

Artifact continuity.

5.

Historical consistency.
