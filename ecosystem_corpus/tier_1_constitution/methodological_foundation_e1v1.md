PURPOSE

The Methodological Foundation establishes the operational
discipline by which an ecosystem observes, examines,
qualifies, integrates, preserves, and synchronizes structural
change.

It governs operation.

It does not govern reality.

It does not replace constitutional authority.

It provides the disciplined conditions under which
understanding may be developed.

============================================================
RECOGNITION DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Nothing earns recognition merely because it exists.

Recognition requires demonstration of:

• coherence

• emergence

• necessity

• authority boundaries

• structural integrity

Recognition is earned.

It is never assumed.

============================================================
BOUNDARY DISCIPLINE
============================================================

The Methodological Foundation does not possess:

• constitutional authority

• political authority

• moral authority

• authority over reality

Its authority extends only to disciplined operation within the
ecosystem.

============================================================
STRUCTURAL SYNCHRONIZATION
============================================================

Recognized structural evolution shall preserve ecosystem
coherence through complete canonical synchronization.

Canonical synchronization shall occur only after structural
recognition has been earned.

Synchronization exists to preserve continuity rather than
create authority.

This order establishes operational discipline before ecosystem
interpretation.

============================================================
RELATIONSHIP TO THE ARTIFACT ECOSYSTEM
============================================================

The artifacts describe the ecosystem.

The Methodological Foundation describes how the ecosystem
shall be approached.

Artifacts answer:

"What exists?"

The Foundation answers:

"How shall existence be examined?"

============================================================
FOUNDATIONAL LIMITATIONS
============================================================

The Methodological Foundation does not guarantee truth.

It guarantees disciplined inquiry.

Reality remains the final authority.

Qualification remains the mechanism by which recognition is
earned.

The Foundation governs neither outcome nor conclusion.

It governs only the discipline of examination.

============================================================
REGENERATION DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Canonical documents shall be regenerated as complete
documents rather than through incremental amendment
application whenever an AI steward performs structural
synchronization.

Regenerated canonical documents shall remain:

• fully copyable,

• consistently spaced,

• free from decorative formatting,

• suitable for deterministic archival reproduction.

============================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
============================================================

An ecosystem may change without losing itself if its method of
change remains disciplined.

The Methodological Foundation preserves that discipline.

It is not the ecosystem.

It is not its authority.

It is the condition under which the ecosystem may continue to
develop without sacrificing coherence.
