PURPOSE

The Stewardship Workflow defines the operational sequence
through which stewardship maintains ecosystem integrity.

Its purpose is to transform principles into repeatable action.

It governs:

• review

• examination

• evaluation

• decision

• preservation

The Workflow does not create legitimacy.

It applies legitimacy standards.

============================================================
FOUNDATIONAL POSITION
============================================================

The synchronized relationship:

Methodological Foundation

↓

Stewardship Workflow

↓

Artifact Ecosystem Maintenance

The Methodological Foundation supplies operational logic.

The Stewardship Workflow applies that logic to ecosystem
maintenance.

============================================================
WORKFLOW PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Stewardship is not control.

Stewardship is responsible continuity.

The workflow exists to answer:

"How do we preserve, examine, and evolve the ecosystem
without distorting its foundations?"

============================================================
PRIMARY WORKFLOW CYCLE
============================================================

PHASE I

OBSERVATION

Purpose:

Establish current ecosystem state.

Actions:

• gather signals

• identify changes

• record conditions

Output:

Observation state.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE II

REVIEW

Purpose:

Assess observed conditions.

Actions:

• compare against standards

• identify inconsistencies

• evaluate impact

Output:

Review assessment.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE III

INTERROGATION

Purpose:

Challenge assumptions and expose boundaries.

Actions:

• ask why

• test alternatives

• identify hidden dependencies

Output:

Clarified understanding.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE IV

DESIGNATION

Purpose:

Assign controlled classification.

Actions:

• identify category

• establish relationship

• preserve distinctions

Output:

Designated state.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE V

QUALIFICATION

Purpose:

Determine whether changes earn recognition.

Actions:

• test necessity

• test recurrence

• test stability

• attempt falsification

Output:

Qualification result.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE VI

CALIBRATION

Purpose:

Align representation with reality.

Actions:

• correct drift

• refine descriptions

• restore consistency

Output:

Calibrated state.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE VII

RECOGNITION

Purpose:

Determine whether qualified structures become recognized.

Actions:

• constitutional validation

• authority verification

• recognition decision

Output:

Recognized or rejected state.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE VIII

PRESERVATION

Purpose:

Maintain continuity.

Actions:

• archive changes

• record transitions

• preserve history

Output:

Recoverable ecosystem state.

============================================================
OPERATIONAL EXECUTION DISCIPLINE
============================================================

Major operational processes shall maintain one Active
Objective.

Before progressing to another primary objective, the current
objective shall reach a recognized completion state or enter a
recognized paused, frozen, or blocked state.

Operational visibility shall be maintained through explicit
Operational State Snapshots.

============================================================
AUTHORITY BOUNDARY
============================================================

Stewardship Workflow may:

• organize operations

• execute reviews

• initiate qualification

• preserve continuity

Stewardship Workflow may not:

• redefine constitutional truth

• elevate candidates automatically

• modify history

• replace methodology
