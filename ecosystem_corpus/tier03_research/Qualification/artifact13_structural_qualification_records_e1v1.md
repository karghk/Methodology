PURPOSE

Structural Qualification Records preserve the formal evidence
required to determine whether a structure earns recognition.

Its purpose is to maintain:

• qualification processes

• examination results

• acceptance decisions

• rejection decisions

• recognition boundaries


Structural Qualification Records do not create legitimacy.

They preserve the reasoning by which legitimacy is determined.

============================================================
QUALIFICATION RECORD STRUCTURE
============================================================


COMPONENT I

SUBJECT IDENTIFICATION


Purpose:

Define what is undergoing qualification.


Contains:

• candidate or structure name

• origin reference

• examination scope

• qualification category


------------------------------------------------------------


COMPONENT II

COMPOSITION RECORD


Purpose:

Determine whether internal structure exists.


Questions:

• Are components related?

• Are transformations present?

• Are boundaries defined?

• Is there internal organization?


Result:

Structural coherence assessment.


------------------------------------------------------------


COMPONENT III

EMERGENCE RECORD


Purpose:

Determine origin relationship.


Questions:

• Was the structure imposed?

• Did it emerge naturally?

• Does it precede recognition?


Result:

Origin classification.


------------------------------------------------------------


COMPONENT IV

NECESSITY RECORD


Purpose:

Determine functional importance.


Questions:

• Does removal damage coherence?

• Does the structure explain relationships?

• Is it required for understanding?


Result:

Necessity assessment.


------------------------------------------------------------


COMPONENT V

AUTHORITY BOUNDARY RECORD


Purpose:

Determine lawful placement.


Questions:

• Is it an artifact?

• Is it a foundation?

• Is it external?

• What limits apply?


Result:

Authority classification.


------------------------------------------------------------


COMPONENT VI

RECOGNITION DECISION


Purpose:

Record final determination.


States:

• recognized

• rejected

• provisional

• preserved for future review
