PURPOSE

Research Programs organize structured exploration of unknown,
uncertain, or unresolved subjects within the ecosystem.

Their purpose is to preserve:

• inquiry continuity

• investigation structure

• unresolved questions

• research pathways

• discovery records

Research Programs do not establish truth.

They create disciplined environments for discovering whether
truth can be established.
