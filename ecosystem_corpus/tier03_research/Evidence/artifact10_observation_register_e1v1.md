PURPOSE

The Observation Register preserves direct observations before
higher-level interpretation, designation, or qualification.

Its purpose is to maintain:

• observation integrity

• evidence traceability

• contextual grounding

• separation between reality and interpretation

The Observation Register preserves contact with reality.

It does not determine meaning.
