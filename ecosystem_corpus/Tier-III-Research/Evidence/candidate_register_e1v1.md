PURPOSE

The Candidate Register preserves unresolved possibilities
within the ecosystem.

Its purpose is to maintain:

• candidate structures

• hypotheses

• emerging patterns

• unresolved interpretations

• qualification readiness

The Candidate Register prevents premature recognition.

It protects possibility without confusing possibility with
truth.
