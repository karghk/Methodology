PURPOSE

The Session Records preserve operational continuity between
stewardship sessions.

Their purpose is to ensure that work resumes from the latest
recognized ecosystem state without reconstructing prior
reasoning.

Session Records preserve continuity.

They do not redefine the ecosystem.

They do not modify authority.

They do not replace the artifacts.


============================================================
SESSION PRINCIPLE
============================================================


A session is temporary.

The ecosystem is persistent.


Every session inherits the current synchronized ecosystem
before continuing stewardship.


============================================================

============================================================
SESSION CONTINUITY
============================================================


Each session shall assume:


• the Methodological Foundation is current,

• the Atlas reflects the recognized topology,

• the Artifact Registry reflects the canonical inventory,

• the artifacts represent the current synchronized ecosystem,

• the current session extends rather than recreates prior
  stewardship.



============================================================
SESSION BOUNDARIES
============================================================


A session may:


• continue stewardship,

• perform observation,

• qualify candidates,

• synchronize recognized changes.



A session shall never:


• alter constitutional authority,

• bypass qualification,

• overwrite recognized artifacts without synchronization,

• treat temporary reasoning as canonical.

RECOVERY
============================================================

available.
