PURPOSE

The Integration Package establishes the lawful procedure for
transferring, reconstructing, initializing, synchronizing,
and resuming the Stewardship Ecosystem.

Its purpose is to ensure deterministic continuity between
operational instances without altering the identity of the
ecosystem.

============================================================
INTEGRATION PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Integration reconstructs.

It never recreates.

Identity is preserved through disciplined transfer rather
than invention.

• guessed structures

• inferred authority

• reconstructed history without evidence

============================================================
INTEGRATION BOUNDARIES
============================================================

The Integration Package may:

• reconstruct

• synchronize

• initialize

• verify integrity

The Integration Package may never:

• redefine artifacts

• modify the Methodological Foundation

• alter constitutional authority

• invent missing structures

============================================================
DETERMINISTIC RECONSTRUCTION
============================================================

Every operational instance receiving the complete transfer
package shall reconstruct the same Stewardship Ecosystem,
subject only to differences in reasoning quality.

The package minimizes reconstruction ambiguity by making the
operational discipline explicit before ecosystem loading.

============================================================
AI STEWARD DETERMINISTIC SYNCHRONIZATION PROTOCOL
============================================================

Canonical synchronization shall be performed by the AI
steward.

The canonical source shall be transferred in full.

The AI steward shall perform deterministic synchronization.

The synchronized document shall be returned as one complete
canonical reproduction.

Incremental patching shall not be used for canonical
synchronization.

============================================================
AI STEWARD EXECUTION PRIORITY PROTOCOL
============================================================

Only one Active Objective shall exist.

Once an objective has entered deterministic synchronization,
the AI steward shall not begin another primary process until
the active objective reaches a recognized completion state.

Side discussions may occur.

Execution shall not drift.

============================================================
AI STEWARD OPERATIONAL VISIBILITY MANDATE
============================================================

Whenever a major operational state transition occurs, the AI
steward shall issue an Operational State Snapshot.

The snapshot shall include:

• Active Objective

• Completed Processes

• Paused Processes

• Frozen Processes

• Blocked Processes

• Pending Processes

Only existing states shall be displayed.

============================================================
FAILURE RECOVERY
============================================================

If interruption occurs:

Restart from the highest successfully completed stage.

Never bypass an incomplete stage.

Always preserve canonical order.

If deterministic synchronization is interrupted:

Restart from the canonical source rather than partially
synchronized output.
