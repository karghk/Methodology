PURPOSE

The Artifact Registry establishes the canonical inventory of
the Stewardship Ecosystem.

It identifies every recognized document, subsystem,
artifact, its classification, its authority domain, and its
relationship to the ecosystem.

The Registry inventories.

It does not govern.

It does not qualify.

It does not preserve.
