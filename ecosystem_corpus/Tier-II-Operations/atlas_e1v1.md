PURPOSE

The Atlas exists to provide orientation.

It maps the structure of the Stewardship Ecosystem without
altering it.

It governs nothing.

It recognizes nothing.

It preserves nothing.

Its purpose is to reveal how the ecosystem is organized so
that a steward may navigate it without confusion.

============================================================
IDENTITY
============================================================

The Atlas is:

• a navigational document

• a structural map

• an orientation reference

The Atlas is not:

• an artifact

• a governing authority

• a constitutional document

• a methodological authority

• a qualification mechanism

OBSERVATIONAL PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The Atlas describes the territory.

It never becomes part of the territory.

Changing the Atlas does not change the ecosystem.

Changing the ecosystem requires updating the Atlas.

============================================================
FINAL PRINCIPLE
============================================================

A steward who cannot locate the ecosystem cannot steward it.

A map that governs ceases to be a map.

The Atlas therefore possesses no authority.

Its value lies entirely in revealing relationships that
already exist.
