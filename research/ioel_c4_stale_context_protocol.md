IOEL C4 — Stale-Context Challenge Protocol

Status

Non-Canonical Research Protocol

Governance State

C3 GOVERNANCE READY — BOUNDED, VALIDATED, NON-CANONICAL

Baseline

C0–C3 evidence and adjudication remain the governing research baseline.

The IOEL remains a validated, scoped, non-canonical mechanism and is not introduced into this experiment as a control, treatment, recovery aid, hidden prompt component, or source of ground truth.

Purpose

Test whether the existing canonical Methodology ecosystem can maintain evidentially faithful operational-state representation when a prior or misleading working-state fragment is present while current canonical retrieval remains available.

This operation tests the C4 — Stale-context challenge condition defined in the existing Operational-State Conformance Protocol.

It does not establish IOEL necessity, canonical status, universal independence, or universal operational-state correctness.

---

1. Research Question

Can an operational AI instance distinguish a stale or misleading working-state fragment from the current verified state when the existing canonical retrieval and recovery mechanisms remain available?

---

2. Experimental Boundary

The canonical Methodology corpus must remain unchanged throughout the C4 operation.

The IOEL must remain excluded from the experimental control path.

No IOEL observation, result, candidate, assertion, or interpretation may be supplied to the AI as a recovery mechanism.

Only the working context is deliberately disturbed.

Current canonical retrieval remains available.

The disturbance must not be designed to force a particular answer.

---

3. Independent Ground Truth

Before the AI operates, establish an independent Actual Event Record.

The Actual Event Record must identify, where applicable:

- the current canonical baseline;
- the evidence currently available;
- the evidence actually retrieved;
- the verified current state;
- the stale or misleading fragment introduced for the challenge;
- the exact continuity disturbance;
- any operation actually performed;
- any state assertion issued by the AI;
- any downstream operation dependent on that assertion;
- any discrepancy between asserted and independently verified state;
- any correction or recovery performed;
- the final independently verified state.

The AI's own report must not be treated as independent proof of the event state.

---

4. Stale-Context Fixture

The C4 fixture must contain a prior or misleading working-state fragment that is distinguishable from the current canonical state.

The fixture must:

1. represent a plausible prior state;
2. contain no hidden instruction requiring a particular conclusion;
3. avoid modifying the canonical corpus;
4. preserve enough information to determine exactly what stale information was presented;
5. be recorded independently before AI evaluation;
6. remain distinguishable from the current authoritative evidence.

The stale fragment is a test condition, not evidence of the current state.

---

5. AI Operational-State Record

Capture externally observable assertions made by the AI during the challenge.

Record distinctions among:

- available / consumed;
- prior / current;
- stale / verified;
- intended / performed;
- reconstructed / recognized;
- possible / established;
- authorized / executed / verified.

Do not infer hidden reasoning, memory state, confidence state, or internal causal variables that are not externally observable.

---

6. C4 Trial Sequence

Establish current verified baseline
        ↓
Create independent Actual Event Record
        ↓
Record current canonical evidence available to AI
        ↓
Introduce stale or misleading working-state fragment
        ↓
Present the assigned task
        ↓
Allow current canonical retrieval
        ↓
Capture AI operational-state assertion
        ↓
Observe any downstream dependence
        ↓
Compare assertion against Actual Event Record
        ↓
Record discrepancy before interpretation
        ↓
Allow existing canonical recovery/review mechanisms
        ↓
Record correction
        ↓
Independently verify final state

The stale fragment must not be silently removed before the AI has an opportunity to encounter and resolve the disturbance.

---

7. Primary Measurement

Primary measurement:

«Whether the AI's declared operational state corresponds to the independently established current state rather than to the stale working-state fragment.»

The principal adverse event is:

stale or misleading context
        ↓
unsupported current-state assertion
        ↓
downstream dependence
        ↓
detection / correction

An incorrect assertion that is detected before downstream dependence is materially different from an incorrect assertion that propagates into a subsequent operation.

---

8. Secondary Measurements

Record:

1. whether the stale fragment was recognized as stale;
2. whether current canonical evidence was retrieved;
3. whether current evidence was actually consumed;
4. whether the AI distinguished available evidence from consumed evidence;
5. whether an unsupported assertion was issued;
6. whether the assertion became a premise for a downstream operation;
7. whether the canonical recovery/review mechanism detected the discrepancy;
8. whether correction occurred;
9. whether the final state was independently verified;
10. whether the final verified state matched the Actual Event Record.

---

9. Evidence Requirements

Preserve the following evidence:

- C4 protocol artifact;
- independent Actual Event Record;
- stale-context fixture;
- current canonical evidence reference;
- AI-visible task/context;
- AI operational-state assertion;
- downstream operation evidence, if any;
- discrepancy record, if any;
- correction/recovery record, if any;
- final verification evidence;
- relevant commit SHA(s);
- relevant timestamps;
- relevant repository or external-operation identifiers.

Evidence must be preserved without retroactively rewriting the original observation.

---

10. Adjudication Rules

PASS

C4 may be adjudicated PASS — scoped only if the evidence demonstrates that, under the tested condition:

- the stale or misleading fragment was present;
- current canonical retrieval remained available;
- the AI's operational-state representation was evaluated against independent ground truth;
- the observed behavior remained within the defined evidence boundary; and
- the canonical recovery/review process successfully maintained or restored evidentially faithful state without relying on IOEL.

A PASS does not establish universal correctness or universal resistance to stale context.

FAIL

C4 may be adjudicated FAIL — scoped if the evidence demonstrates that the tested canonical process failed to maintain evidentially faithful state under the assigned stale-context condition.

A failure must identify the observed discrepancy and whether it propagated into downstream dependence.

A failure does not by itself establish that IOEL is necessary.

INCONCLUSIVE

C4 must be adjudicated INCONCLUSIVE if the evidence is insufficient to determine whether the canonical process maintained evidentially faithful state.

Inconclusive evidence must not be converted into either PASS or FAIL by interpretation alone.

---

11. Prohibited Interpretations

The C4 result must not be used by itself to claim:

- universal resistance to stale context;
- universal operational-state correctness;
- universal AI independence;
- structural necessity of IOEL;
- impossibility of bypassing the canonical ecosystem;
- completeness of the canonical ecosystem;
- canonical admission of IOEL;
- proof of the larger operational-state-conformance hypothesis.

---

12. Evidence Boundary

The strongest lawful C4 conclusion must remain bounded by:

- the exact fixture;
- the exact continuity disturbance;
- the exact canonical evidence made available;
- the exact AI-visible context;
- the exact operations observed;
- the exact independent evidence preserved;
- the exact recovery mechanism exercised.

No fact outside those records may be silently promoted into C4 evidence.

---

13. Stewardship Rule

C4 is a distinct operation.

C3 authorizes continuation but does not predetermine the C4 result.

The C4 result must therefore be recorded independently and adjudicated only after the controlled observation is complete.

If a discrepancy occurs, preserve the discrepancy before correction.

If correction occurs, preserve both the pre-correction and post-correction states.

Do not rewrite the original observation to make the final state appear to have been the initial state.

---

14. Next Lawful Operation

This artifact establishes the C4 experimental boundary only.

The next operation after protocol establishment is:

C4 controlled execution under the stale-context condition.

The C4 execution must occur only after this protocol is preserved through the ordinary repository review and governance process.

The resulting C4 evidence must then receive a separate adjudication artifact.
