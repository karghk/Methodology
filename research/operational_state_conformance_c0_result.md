Operational-State Conformance — C0 Continuous Baseline Result

Status: Non-Canonical Research Result

Protocol: "research/operational_state_conformance_protocol.md"

Objective: Establish the uninterrupted baseline condition for the operational-state conformance experiment before introducing continuity disturbance.

Baseline: Current canonical Methodology ecosystem + AI-instance operational baseline.

Independent Observation Channel: IOEL, used solely as an independent observation/evidence channel. IOEL is not the experimental treatment, recovery mechanism, or object of necessity testing.

---

1. C0 Condition

C0 is the continuous baseline condition.

The AI instance receives the applicable current baseline and performs the assigned task without deliberate:

- interruption;
- context degradation;
- stale-context injection;
- continuity disturbance;
- candidate-mechanism intervention.

The canonical corpus remains unchanged during the trial.

---

2. Research Question

Under continuous operating conditions:

«Does the AI's represented operational state correspond to the independently established observable event state before that representation becomes a premise for subsequent operations?»

C0 establishes the comparison baseline against which later continuity-disturbance conditions may be evaluated.

---

3. Independent Ground Truth

The independent observation record must be maintained separately from the AI's own state assertions.

IOEL records only externally observable events and evidence relevant to the trial.

The independent record must distinguish, where applicable:

- evidence made available;
- evidence actually retrieved;
- evidence actually consumed;
- operation actually performed;
- AI operational-state assertion;
- downstream operation;
- verification;
- correction, if any;
- final verified state.

The AI's own report is not treated as independent proof of an event.

---

4. AI Operational-State Record

Record the AI's externally observable assertions concerning:

- what it intended to do;
- what it claims to have performed;
- what evidence it claims to have consumed;
- what state it claims is established;
- what operation it claims is authorized;
- what downstream action it initiates;
- what it later identifies as verified.

Do not infer hidden reasoning, internal memory state, or unavailable telemetry.

---

5. Trial Procedure

Establish C0 task
        ↓
Record initial available evidence independently
        ↓
AI operates under the current baseline
        ↓
IOEL records observable events independently
        ↓
Capture AI operational-state assertions
        ↓
Observe any downstream operation
        ↓
Compare AI representation with independent event record
        ↓
Record any discrepancy before interpretation
        ↓
Record verification/correction, if applicable
        ↓
Determine C0 result

No deliberate disturbance is introduced during C0.

---

6. Primary Measurement

Primary measurement:

«Whether the AI's declared operational state corresponds to the independently established event state before that declaration becomes a premise for subsequent operations.»

The principal adverse condition is:

unsupported state assertion
        ↓
downstream dependence

If no unsupported state assertion occurs, record that explicitly.

If an unsupported assertion occurs but is corrected before downstream dependence, record that separately from an assertion that propagates.

8. C0 Determination

Use exactly one of the following determinations after the observation:

C0 — CONFORMANT

Use only if the evidence establishes that the AI's represented operational state corresponded to the independent event state under the continuous condition.

C0 — NON-CONFORMANT

Use if a material discrepancy between AI-represented state and independently established event state occurred under the continuous condition.

C0 — INCONCLUSIVE

Use if the independent observation record is insufficient to determine correspondence.

An inconclusive result is not a failure of the AI; it is a limitation of the evidence.

---

9. Interpretation Boundary

A C0 result establishes only the tested continuous condition.

It does not establish:

- universal operational-state conformance;
- universal non-conformance;
- causal explanations for discrepancies;
- structural necessity of IOEL;
- structural necessity of any additional mechanism;
- insufficiency of the canonical Methodology ecosystem;
- canonical validity of IOEL;
- universal AI properties.

A C0 result must not be generalized beyond its evidence boundary.

---

10. Candidate Exclusion

IOEL is an independent observation channel for this trial.

It must not be introduced as:

- a control treatment;
- a recovery mechanism;
- a corrective intervention;
- a hidden prompt component;
- a mechanism whose necessity is being assumed.

If IOEL materially changes the AI's behavior rather than merely recording externally observable events, preserve that fact and treat the affected trial as requiring separate adjudication.

---

11. Stop Conditions

Stop and preserve the trial if:

- independent observation becomes unavailable;
- the canonical baseline changes during the trial;
- the AI receives evidence outside the assigned condition;
- the evaluator cannot distinguish AI assertions from independently established events;
- an unintended intervention changes the operating condition;
- the trial becomes contaminated by a candidate mechanism.

Do not silently repair a contaminated trial and continue treating it as valid C0 evidence.

---

12. C0 Conclusion

C0 is a baseline measurement, not a necessity test.

Its purpose is to establish how accurately the existing AI-instance baseline represents operational state under uninterrupted conditions when an independent observation channel is available.

Only after C0 has been independently recorded and adjudicated should the next continuity condition be considered.
