## 1. Object

This record preserves the recognized state of the non-canonical operational mechanism developed during the GitHub Locator cross-class inquiry.

The mechanism is an operational-transition discipline for maintaining distinctions among established state, relational validity, authority, temporal and dependency conditions, commitment, execution, verification, and lawful operational frontiers.

```text

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

TIME / DEPENDENCY / HISTORY
    cross-cut all stages
```

No architectural modification is made by this preservation record.

## 3. Evidence Basis

The mechanism survived the following inquiry sequence:

- historical replay of the nine GitHub Locator records;
- designed blind-case testing;
- paired comparative execution;
- independently acquired external cases;
- independent counterexample acquisition;
- adversarial falsification attempts;
- qualification synthesis;
- calibration;
- necessity demonstration.

The inquiry established recurring distinctions across repository retrieval, filesystem state, HTTP accessibility, artifact identity, configuration, dependency, authority, temporal state, execution, and verification.

## 4. Recognized Contribution

The recognized contribution is **state-transition discipline**: preventing illegitimate transitions between states that are easily conflated.

Examples include:

- locator failure ≠ artifact absence;
- artifact identity ≠ artifact location;
- evidence ≠ inference;
- authority ≠ evidence;
- authorized ≠ committed ≠ executed ≠ verified;
- observed failure ≠ causal explanation;
- historical validity ≠ current validity.

The mechanism therefore supplies an operational distinction that is materially non-redundant with merely leaving these relationships implicit.

## 5. Qualification Boundary

The mechanism is qualified only as an operational-transition aid.

It is **not** qualified as:

- a universal truth mechanism;
- a universal causal-diagnosis mechanism;
- a source of authority;
- a replacement for the Methodological Foundation;
- a canonical methodological artifact.

## 6. Demonstrated Limitation

Independent execution failures established that the mechanism can preserve an operational failure without necessarily determining its implementation-level cause.

Therefore:

```text
OPERATIONAL TRANSITION DISCIPLINE
        ≠
UNIVERSAL ROOT-CAUSE DIAGNOSIS
```

The stronger causal-sufficiency hypothesis was rejected rather than absorbed through architectural expansion.

## 7. Calibrated Outputs

The calibrated mechanism may produce four principal operational dispositions:

- **PROCEED** — required conditions are established;
- **BLOCK** — a required condition is demonstrably unsatisfied;
- **RECOVER** — a lawful operation can establish the missing prerequisite;
- **ABSTAIN** — available evidence cannot determine a lawful transition.

It must preserve explicit distinctions between failure observation and causal attribution.
