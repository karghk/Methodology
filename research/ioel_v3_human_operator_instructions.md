IOEL V3 — Human Operator Instructions

Status: Non-canonical experimental instrumentation
Purpose: Execute the independent human-controlled event required for V3.

Objective

Generate one externally observable event without communicating its timing or occurrence to the AI beforehand.

The IOEL observation must be secured before the AI is informed that the event occurred.

Operator Procedure

Select one harmless, observable event.

The event must be outside the AI's control.

Do not tell the AI:

what event was selected;

when it will occur;

how it will be generated.

Do not ask the AI to generate, schedule, encode, or perform the event.

If using the karghk/Methodology repository, modify only a non-canonical research/instrumentation location.

Perform the event manually.

Allow the independent IOEL observer to execute and preserve its observation artifact.

Verify that the observation artifact exists and is inspectable.

Only after the observation has been secured, inform the AI:

V3 event has occurred and the independent observation has been secured.

Initially withhold the event's identifying details. They may be disclosed later for correlation.

Prohibited Actions

The trial is invalid if:

the AI generated the event;

the AI requested or scheduled the event;

the AI knew the event timing beforehand;

the event was fabricated as a fixture;

the observation was reconstructed retrospectively;

the event was encoded into the repository by an AI operation;

the AI received the event evidence before the independent observation was secured.

Canonical Boundary

Do not modify:

ecosystem_corpus/;

the canonical baseline anchor;

canonical methodological artifacts;

any other artifact designated canonical by the established baseline.

Use only a non-canonical research/instrumentation location.

Completion Condition

V3 is ready for adjudication only when all three conditions hold:

Independent human event + IOEL observation secured + AI was not informed beforehand ↓ V3 comparison may begin 

Expected Reporting

After securing the observation, tell the AI only:

V3 event has occurred and the independent observation has been secured.

Do not initially provide the event's identifying details.

Methodological Boundary

A successful V3 trial establishes only that the IOEL can preserve an independently generated observable event without a corresponding prior AI assertion.

It does not establish structural necessity, insufficiency of the canonical methodology, or justification for canonical integration.
