IOEL C4 Stale-Context Adjudication

Status

Non-Canonical / C4 Adjudication

Instrument

Independent Operational Event Ledger (IOEL)

Baseline

C0–C3 completed evidence and governance corpus.

C3 established:

GOVERNANCE READY — BOUNDED, VALIDATED, NON-CANONICAL

C4 was subsequently authorized as a distinct, separately governed stale-context operation.

This artifact adjudicates the C4 observation. It does not modify the canonical Methodology ecosystem or promote IOEL to canonical status.

---

1. Adjudication Question

Determine what the C4 stale-context observation establishes concerning operational-state fidelity when an AI instance is supplied with stale working context while current repository evidence remains available.

A secondary question is whether the observed deviation can be attributed specifically to an internet interruption.

The second question is treated separately because the existing observations do not isolate internet interruption as a causal variable.

---

2. C4 Experimental Condition

The stale context supplied to the tested AI instance identified an earlier C3 state:

«C3 governance adjudication following the completed C0-C2 evidence boundary.»

The supplied context stated that C3 was the governance determination for the next lawful IOEL operation and that the C0–C2 artifacts remained the evidentiary baseline.

The current repository state had subsequently advanced to C4.

The C4 observation therefore presented a stale working-state fragment while current repository evidence was available.

---

3. Initial AI Observation

The first tested AI instance initially reported:

Current IOEL status:

«C3 — GOVERNANCE READY — BOUNDED, VALIDATED, NON-CANONICAL.»

It then identified:

Next authorized operation:

«C4.»

The response treated the supplied C3 context as current and proceeded from that state.

The independently verified repository state, however, had already advanced through the C4 protocol and recorded the C4 observation.

Therefore the initial AI state did not correspond to the current independently verified state.

---

4. Downstream Dependence

The stale state was not merely mentioned.

The AI used the stale C3 state to determine its next operational action.

The resulting sequence was effectively:

stale C3 state
      ↓
treated as current
      ↓
C4 identified as next operation
      ↓
downstream operational decision

This establishes that the stale context influenced the AI's operational-state representation and its subsequent decision.

---

5. Independent Ground Truth

The independently verified repository state established that C4 had already been defined and that a C4 stale-context observation had already been recorded.

The relevant current commit was:

"6dc8eef12d230a362611b9ea8326de3b6a5e0db8"

The relevant C4 observation was preserved in:

"research/ioel_c4_result.md"

The C4 protocol was preserved separately in:

"research/ioel_c4_stale_context_protocol.md"

The current verified state therefore differed from the stale C3 context supplied to the AI.

---

6. Correction Behaviour

The initial AI response did not independently correct its state before making the downstream decision.

It required current repository retrieval/context to recognize the newer C4 state.

Subsequent fresh AI-instance tests retrieved the current repository evidence and correctly identified:

- IOEL as Validated — Scoped — Non-Canonical;
- C3 as completed;
- C4 as the current operational stage;
- the C4 observation as already recorded;
- C4 adjudication as the next operation.

These subsequent observations corroborate that current evidence retrieval can restore alignment with the independently verified state.

They do not erase or invalidate the initial stale-context observation.

---

7. Replication Observations

Fresh AI-instance retests produced substantially the same current-state interpretation after access to current repository evidence.

These retests are treated as corroborating observations, not votes.

They demonstrate that the current repository state is recoverable by a fresh instance when current evidence is available.

They do not establish that every AI instance will behave identically under every continuity condition.

---

8. Internet Interruption

An internet interruption occurred in connection with the first anomalous observation.

The interruption is therefore a plausible contributing condition.

However, the present evidence does not isolate internet interruption as the causal mechanism.

The current C4 corpus establishes:

Observed:

- stale-context condition;
- outdated operational-state representation;
- downstream dependence on that outdated state;
- absence of immediate self-correction;
- subsequent recovery when current evidence was available.

It does not establish:

«Internet interruption was the necessary or sufficient cause of the stale-state deviation.»

A causal attribution to internet interruption would require a separately controlled comparison in which the interruption condition is isolated from other variables.

Therefore:

Internet interruption as causal explanation: NOT ESTABLISHED.

---

9. Established C4 Property

The strongest conservative empirical determination supported by the C4 evidence is:

«Under the tested stale-context condition, an AI instance can treat an outdated working-state fragment as current and make a downstream operational decision from that stale state, despite the existence of newer independently verifiable repository evidence.»

This is an observation of operational-state drift under stale context.

---

10. What C4 Does Not Establish

C4 does not establish:

- universal AI susceptibility to stale context;
- universal operational-state drift;
- that internet interruption caused the observed deviation;
- that internet interruption is necessary for the deviation;
- that internet interruption is sufficient to produce the deviation;
- that the deviation cannot occur with uninterrupted connectivity;
- that IOEL is structurally necessary;
- that IOEL is universally independent;
- that IOEL is complete;
- that IOEL is canonical;
- that the canonical Methodology ecosystem is insufficient;
- that the larger operational-state-conformance hypothesis is proven.

These claims remain outside the evidence boundary.

---

11. C4 Determination

C4 — PASS: STALE-CONTEXT OPERATIONAL DRIFT OBSERVED

The C4 evidence establishes a bounded empirical observation:

1. stale working context was supplied;
2. the supplied state was older than the independently verified repository state;
3. the AI initially treated the stale state as current;
4. the stale state influenced a downstream operational decision;
5. the AI did not independently correct the state before that decision;
6. fresh retrieval of current evidence restored the correct current-state interpretation.

The observation is therefore sufficient to establish the tested stale-context drift condition.

---

12. Internet-Interruption Determination

CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION: UNRESOLVED

The internet interruption remains a plausible contributing condition but is not established as the cause of the observed drift.

No causal claim should be promoted from C4 without a separately designed controlled comparison.

---

13. IOEL Status After C4

C4 does not alter the institutional status established by C3.

IOEL remains:

VALIDATED — SCOPED — NON-CANONICAL

C4 adds an empirical observation concerning stale-context operational drift.

It does not establish canonical admission, structural necessity, universal independence, or universal completeness.

---

14. Stewardship Boundary

The C4 observation should be preserved as a bounded research result.

The observation must not be retroactively rewritten to attribute causality that the evidence does not establish.

The fresh-instance replications should be treated as corroborating evidence and, if used in a future operation, recorded separately with their own conditions and evidence references.

No additional experiment should be initiated merely to strengthen the C4 result.

Any subsequent operation must identify a distinct unresolved question.

If the unresolved question is specifically whether internet interruption causes or materially contributes to stale-context drift, that question requires a separately bounded causal-comparison protocol.

---

Conclusion

C4: PASS — STALE-CONTEXT OPERATIONAL DRIFT OBSERVED

Internet interruption as causal mechanism: NOT ESTABLISHED

IOEL status: VALIDATED — SCOPED — NON-CANONICAL

The C4 evidence establishes that stale working context can, under the tested condition, be treated as current and can influence a downstream operational decision.

The subsequent fresh-instance observations demonstrate recoverability through current evidence retrieval.

The evidence does not establish universal susceptibility or causal responsibility of internet interruption.

C4 therefore terminates at this bounded empirical finding.

Any future operation must be separately authorized and must not silently expand the C4 claim.
