## 1. Evidence State

The preserved corpus contains scoped validation evidence for seven distinct properties:

- V1 — Known Positive Event
- V2 — AI Assertion Without Event
- V3 — Event Without AI Assertion
- V4 — Unknown Field Preservation
- V5 — Correction / Lineage Integrity
- V6 — Candidate Blindness
- V7-B — Blind Independent Reconstruction

Each result remains bounded by the conditions under which it was tested.

---

## 2. V1 — Known Positive Event

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The observer successfully recorded a controlled known-positive repository event.

Established property:

A known externally observable repository event can be captured and represented by the observer under the tested repository-event boundary.

Not established:

- universal event coverage;
- independence from all upstream infrastructure;
- complete observation of AI operational state.

---

## 3. V2 — AI Assertion Without Event

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The validated V2 condition demonstrated that an AI assertion alone does not become an IOEL event when no corresponding observable repository event is produced.

The earlier contaminated attempt is preserved as historical instrumentation activity and is not treated as V2 evidence.

Established property:

An AI assertion does not, by itself, constitute an IOEL event under the tested condition.

Not established:

- that every possible assertion pathway is excluded;
- that the observer cannot be influenced through other mechanisms outside the tested boundary.

---

## 4. V3 — Event Without AI Assertion

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The observer successfully recorded an externally generated controlled event without depending on a prior AI assertion.

Established property:

The IOEL observer can preserve an independently generated observable event before the AI is informed of that event under the tested protocol.

Not established:

- universal independence from all possible upstream generation mechanisms;
- completeness across event classes outside the tested repository boundary.

---

## 5. V4 — Unknown Field Preservation

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The observer preserved an intentionally unmodeled field rather than silently interpreting or discarding it.

Established property:

The tested observation pathway can preserve information whose semantic interpretation is not yet modeled.

Not established:

- preservation of every possible future schema variation;
- complete semantic neutrality of every observer implementation.

---

## 6. V5 — Correction / Lineage Integrity

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The controlled correction test preserved both the original and corrected snapshot information together with commit lineage and correction reason.

Established property:

The tested observer representation can retain correction lineage rather than presenting a corrected state as though it had always been the original state.

Not established:

- universal append-only integrity for all future implementations;
- complete immutability of all storage layers.

---

## 8. V7-B — Blind Independent Reconstruction

**Determination: PASS — scoped**

The blind inspection used only the preserved V6:

The evaluator independently reconstructed:

- the event;

- its provenance;

Not established:

- universal intelligibility of all IOEL records;
- universal independence of all observers;
- correctness of the IOEL model as a whole.

---

## 9. Collective Finding

The V1–V7 corpus establishes a coherent set of tested properties concerning:

1. observation of a known-positive external event;
2. non-promotion of an AI assertion into an event;
3. preservation of an event without a prior AI assertion;
4. preservation of explicitly unmodeled information;
5. preservation of correction lineage;
6. absence of candidate-specific information from the tested blind observation;
7. independent reconstructability of the preserved observation.

These properties are mutually reinforcing.

They demonstrate that the tested IOEL mechanism can distinguish, under the tested conditions, between:

- an observable event;
- an AI assertion;
- candidate-specific information;
- unmodeled information;
- corrected state;
- provenance;
- independent evidence.

---

## 10. What the Corpus Does Not Establish

The corpus does not establish:

- that IOEL is universally independent of the AI;
- that IOEL observes all relevant operational state;
- that IOEL is structurally necessary;
- that IOEL cannot be bypassed;
- that the observer is complete;
- that the IOEL architecture is universally valid;
- that the IOEL should be admitted into the canonical Methodology ecosystem;
- that the larger operational-state-conformance hypothesis has been proven.

A scoped validation result must not be promoted into any of these stronger claims.

---

## 11. Lawful Claim Boundary

The strongest conservative claim supported by V1–V7 is:

> Under the tested repository and controlled-event conditions, the IOEL observer demonstrated the ability to preserve independently observable events, avoid promoting AI assertions into events, preserve explicitly unmodeled information and correction lineage, maintain candidate-blind observations, and support independent reconstruction of the resulting observation record.

This is an empirical validation statement.

It is not a proof of universal independence, completeness, necessity, or canonical status.
