## Status

**Validated — Scoped — Non-Canonical**

## Purpose

Establish the lawful claim boundary for IOEL before any C1–C4 governance operation.

C0 does not introduce a new validation probe, modify the IOEL observer, or admit IOEL into the canonical Methodology ecosystem.

## Established

The V1–V7 validation corpus establishes, under the tested repository and controlled-event conditions, that the IOEL mechanism can:

1. observe a known-positive external repository event;
2. avoid promoting an AI assertion into an event under the tested V2 condition;
3. preserve an externally generated event without a prior AI assertion under the tested V3 condition;
4. preserve explicitly unmodeled information;
5. preserve correction lineage;
6. preserve candidate-blind observations under the tested V6 condition; and
7. support independent reconstruction of the resulting observation record under V7-B.

## Not Established

The corpus does not establish:

- universal AI-independence;
- universal observation completeness;
- universal bypass-resistance;
- structural necessity;
- universal validity of the IOEL architecture;
- canonical admission into Methodology;
- proof of the larger operational-state-conformance hypothesis.

## Claim Boundary

The strongest presently lawful characterization is:

> IOEL is an empirically validated observation mechanism under the tested conditions, with the validation remaining scoped to the tested repository and controlled-event boundaries.

This characterization must not be strengthened into a claim of universal independence, completeness, necessity, or canonical status without separate evidence and governance.

## C0 Determination

**C0 PASS — Claim boundary established.**

IOEL may proceed to C1 as a **validated, scoped, non-canonical instrument**.

No new experimental validation is implied by this determination.

## Transition Rule

C1 must define the specific claim or governance question to be considered next.

C1 must not presume that validation automatically implies admission, necessity, or architectural establishment.
