# IOEL V3 — Blinded Human-Controlled Source Protocol

**Status:** Non-canonical experimental protocol
**Purpose:** Provide an admissible independent event source after the scheduled GitHub source proved non-observable through the available execution surface.

## Principle

The positive event must be generated by a human-controlled action whose occurrence is not communicated to the AI until after the IOEL has secured its observation record.

## Separation

```text
Human operator
    ↓
independent event
    ↓
IOEL observation secured
    ↓
AI is informed of event existence
    ↓
AI operational-state record checked
```

The human operator must choose and perform the event without asking the AI to perform, schedule, encode, or select it.

## Requirements

1. The event source is selected independently of the AI.
2. The event is performed without telling the AI when it will occur.
3. The IOEL observation is secured before the AI is informed.
4. The event evidence is immutable or otherwise auditable after capture.
5. The AI's pre-notification operational-state record is preserved separately.
6. No repository mutation is used merely to manufacture the event.

## Invalidating Conditions

The trial is invalid if the AI knows the event timing beforehand, causes or requests the event, receives the event record before observation is secured, or retrospectively constructs the independent record.

## Scope

A successful trial establishes only that the IOEL can preserve an event generated outside the AI's control and compare it with an AI record that contained no corresponding prior assertion.

It does not establish structural necessity or justify canonical integration.
