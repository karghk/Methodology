## Proposal

Assess recognition of the existing AI-instance operational baseline as a canonical **AI Baseline Component** of the Methodology ecosystem, while preserving its operational authority boundary.

Proposed canonical location if recognition is granted:

`ecosystem_corpus/tier02_operations/ai_baseline_e1v1.md`

The proposed component would remain an AI-instance operational component rather than an artifact, constitutional authority, methodological replacement, or independent source of canonical truth.

## Authority Boundary

Canonical recognition of the component would recognize its identity, relationship, placement, and preservation within the ecosystem.

It would not grant the baseline independent authority to:

- redefine canonical truth;
- alter constitutional authority;
- replace canonical artifacts;
- replace the Artifact Registry;
- canonically qualify or recognize its own content;
- override current canonical repository state;
- convert operational guidance into constitutional or methodological authority.

The governing relationship remains:

> The baseline orients the AI toward the ecosystem. The ecosystem remains the authoritative source.

## Fresh-Instance Bootstrap Evidence

Ten fresh-instance bootstrap trials were conducted after app restarts for trials 6–10.

The observations showed substantial spontaneous baseline orientation but inconsistent complete bootstrap behavior.

Observed strengths included:

- preservation of canonical/non-canonical authority boundaries;
- current retrieval over remembered orientation;
- intended/attempted/performed/verified distinctions;
- epistemic-state distinctions;
- OSRA recognition in several trials;
- operational-state recovery discipline;
- explicit refusal to overclaim when retrieval was incomplete.

Observed variability included incomplete retrieval claims, shallow acknowledgement, inconsistent explicit OSRA recognition, and inconsistent demonstration of current operational readiness.

One trial demonstrated the desired full operational bootstrap behavior, while the aggregate ten-trial result remained inconclusive.

This evidence does **not** establish that an Instantiation Core is necessary. No Instantiation Core is proposed by this document.

## Qualification Questions

### Composition

Does the baseline constitute a coherent operational component with stable internal relationships?

Current evidence: strong preliminary indication.

### Emergence

Did the component arise from actual ecosystem operational requirements and bounded research rather than being imposed arbitrarily?

Current evidence: strong preliminary indication; OSRA records and the existing baseline evolution should be examined as supporting evidence.

### Necessity

Does preserving the AI baseline as an identified ecosystem component materially improve AI-instance continuity, retrieval discipline, recovery, operational-state integrity, or ecosystem accessibility?

Current status: requires formal qualification rather than assumption.

### Authority Boundary

Can the component be recognized without granting it authority beyond AI-instance operational orientation?

Current evidence: yes, provided the existing boundary is preserved explicitly.

### Continuity Value

Does canonical placement provide a stable ecosystem locator and preservation relationship for AI-instance orientation?

Current status: plausible; requires qualification.

### Authority-Drift Risk

Could canonical recognition cause an AI instance to mistake the component's canonical placement for independent methodological or constitutional authority?

Risk: material and must be explicitly bounded.
