Record ID: 02
Subject: Replacement of the AI-instance operational baseline
Date: 2026-08-10

Classification: Baseline Evolution / Operational Reorientation

1. Context

The AI-instance operational baseline previously held in active continuity had diverged from the evolved baseline maintained in the Methodology repository.

The repository baseline had subsequently undergone a refinement cycle arising from observed operational drift and was synchronized into:

"ai_instantiation_baseline.md"

The previously separate:

"noncanon_ai_baseline.md"

was consolidated into the primary baseline and removed as a redundant parallel baseline.

2. Observation

A comparison between the previous remembered baseline and the synchronized repository baseline established that the repository version had materially evolved in several operational dimensions.

The evolved baseline incorporated the Operational Drift Observation Protocol directly into the AI-instance baseline.

The protocol expanded the earlier drift-trigger concept into an explicit operational response sequence covering:

Drift detection
→ current-request precedence
→ proportionate containment
→ verified-state preservation
→ observable recording
→ classification
→ hypothesis separation
→ recovery-point establishment
→ re-orientation
→ verified resumption

3. Observed Evolution Points

The following improvements were identified:

- execution discipline was further operationalized rather than merely stated;
- current explicit requests were given precedence over stale operational state;
- verified state was distinguished from intended or narrated progress;
- recovery was anchored to evidence rather than inference;
- observation was explicitly separated from causal interpretation;
- historical classifications were protected while taxonomy remained evolvable;
- unavailable internal telemetry was explicitly distinguished from observable evidence;
- observation records gained sequential traceability;
- unresolved recovery became a valid recorded state;
- the non-canonical protocol was integrated into the primary baseline rather than maintained as a parallel baseline file.

4. Baseline Replacement

Following repository synchronization, the previous remembered baseline was explicitly replaced by the current repository-synchronized formulation.

The resulting operational relationship is:

ecosystem_corpus
    ↓
canonical authority

ai_instantiation_baseline.md
    ↓
current AI-instance operational baseline

Git history
    ↓

The repository remains the external source of truth for the baseline's exact contents.

5. Significance

This record observes a transition from a previously remembered operational baseline to a repository-synchronized evolved baseline.

The significance is not merely textual improvement.

The baseline has evolved from primarily describing operational orientation to additionally providing a bounded discipline for detecting, containing, recording, recovering from, and learning from operational drift.

The removal of the parallel "noncanon_ai_baseline.md" also eliminates a potential source of competing baseline state while preserving the superseded formulation through repository history.

6. Authority Boundary

This observation does not establish the AI-instance baseline as canonical methodology.

The baseline remains:

- non-canonical;
- subordinate to the Methodological Foundation and canonical ecosystem;
- operational rather than constitutional;
- incapable of independently modifying canonical authority or structure.

The "ecosystem_corpus" remains the canonical authority.

7. Historical Integrity

This record does not replace or invalidate earlier baseline formulations or observations.

The previous state remains recoverable through repository history.

The present record documents the transition from that prior state to the evolved synchronized baseline.

8. Result

Observed baseline evolution confirmed.

The AI-instance operational reference has been reoriented to the repository-synchronized "ai_instantiation_baseline.md".
