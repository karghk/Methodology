1. Operational Context

The active operation was the methodological re-examination and subsequent canonicalization composition of the AI-Instance Operational Baseline.

The established requirement was to:

- preserve the existing non-canonical baseline as the source/reference state;
- subject the complete baseline to Methodology;
- externalize OSRA as a separately governed operational mechanism;
- externalize the Operational Drift Observation Protocol as a separately governed operational mechanism;
- preserve the remaining baseline-native material;
- compose a complete first canonicalization candidate;
- use e1/v1 because the baseline had not previously undergone canonical revision;
- avoid canonical synchronization until the candidate had undergone qualification and recognition.

The user explicitly required whole-file composition rather than micro-patching or summarization.

2. Expected Operation

The expected operation was:

Complete existing AI-Instance Operational Baseline
→ methodological examination
→ identify OSRA-dependent material
→ identify Operational Drift Observation Protocol-dependent material
→ preserve baseline-native material
→ make only necessary boundary corrections
→ compose a complete whole-file e1/v1 candidate
→ subject the candidate to further Methodology

The resulting candidate was expected to remain substantially faithful to the source in scope and content, except where material was legitimately externalized or boundary corrections were required.

Canonicalization was not to be treated as summarization.

3. Actual Operation

A candidate composition was produced that was drastically shorter than the supplied baseline.

The supplied baseline was approximately one thousand lines without the two protocols.

The produced candidate was fewer than one hundred lines.

The candidate therefore did not constitute a faithful whole-file canonicalization composition.

Instead, substantial baseline material was silently omitted or condensed.

The operation consequently deviated from the established preservation-first requirement.

4. Observable Evidence

The user identified that:

- the source baseline was close to 1,000 lines without OSRA and the Observation Protocol;
- the produced composition did not reach 100 lines;
- the resulting composition therefore could not represent the complete source under canonicalization.

The assistant subsequently acknowledged:

“I drifted.”

The assistant further identified that it had:

- treated an incomplete retrieval as sufficient for composition;
- produced a drastically compressed composition;
- silently rewritten/reduced the baseline;
- failed to perform whole-file canonicalization.
